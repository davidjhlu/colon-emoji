# :emoji privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

:emoji is a Chrome extension for typing emoji with colon shortcodes such as `:smile:`. This policy explains what it handles. The short version: your text is checked on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

## What the extension reads

After you agree on the first-run page, the extension reads the text just before your cursor in text boxes on websites you visit, so it can spot shortcodes. This can include private messages, emails and anything else you type. It doesn't run in password fields, and it doesn't run at all until you agree.

This text is processed on your device, only at the moment you type. It is not saved, and it is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

## What the extension saves

On your device, in the extension's local storage:

- Custom emoji images you upload, and their names
- How many times you've used each emoji, so frequent ones rank higher
- Whether you agreed on the first-run page

In Chrome sync storage:

- Whether the extension is turned on
- The list of sites you turned it off for

If you use Chrome sync, Google syncs these two settings across your signed-in browsers through your Google account, under Google's own privacy policy. The developer can't see them.

## Sharing

The extension has no server, makes no network requests, and has no analytics or ads. The developer doesn't collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

When you choose a custom emoji, the extension attaches that image to the website's text box, as if you had pasted it. What happens to it next is up to you and that website, in the same way as any image you send.

## Your choices

- Turn the extension off everywhere, or on one site, from the toolbar icon
- Delete custom emoji from the Custom emoji page
- Remove the extension to delete everything it stored on your device

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If what the extension does with your data changes, the extension will tell you and ask again.
