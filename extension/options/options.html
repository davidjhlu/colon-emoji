    Good to know

    • Images are shrunk to 64px, which Messenger shows at about that size.
    • Transparent areas may show as white once sent, so tightly cropped images look best.
    • GIFs keep their animation, but Messenger makes people click to play them.
    • Your emoji are stored only in this browser and don't sync to other computers.
