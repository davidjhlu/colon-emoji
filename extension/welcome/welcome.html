Before you start

:emoji is off until you agree to how it works.

What it reads

To spot shortcodes like :smile:, it reads the text just before your cursor as you type in text boxes on the websites you visit. That can include private messages, emails and anything else you type. Password fields are skipped.

What happens to it

  • Your text is checked on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • The extension has no server and makes no network requests.

What it saves

  • On this device: custom emoji images you upload, and how often you use each emoji.
  • In Chrome sync storage: the on/off setting and the list of sites you turned it off for. If you use Chrome sync, Google syncs these through your account.

You're all set

Refresh any tabs that were already open, then type :smile: or :sm in a text box. You can turn it off at any time from the toolbar icon.

:emoji is staying off

It won't read anything. To change your mind, click the toolbar icon and choose Finish setup, or remove the extension from chrome://extensions.
